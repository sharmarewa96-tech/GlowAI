## Brand & Style

The design system projects an aura of understated prestige, sensory indulgence, and editorial authority. Informed by modern beauty ateliers—where clinical efficacy meets tactile decadence—the visual personality is intimate, luminous, and deliberate. The interface behaves like the digital translation of high-end skincare packaging: warm, radiant, and uncluttered.

The target audience seeks personalized aesthetic intelligence without juvenile ornamentation or cold, robotic clinicalism. The UI balances human editorial discernment with algorithmic precision, evoking composure, tactile softness, and effortless beauty.

The design movement synthesizes **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Tactile Soft-Glassmorphism**:
- Generous, serene negative space that lets products, shade formulations, and bespoke recommendations breathe.
- Translucent, frosted ivory overlays reminiscent of frosted flacons and heavy glass cosmetic jars.
- Polished typographic tension between an expressive, high-contrast serif and a crisp, geometric sans-serif.
- Restraint over excess: ornamentation is strictly limited to delicate hairline borders, champagne micro-accents, and subtle rose-tinted ambient luminescence.

## Colors

The palette avoids clinical stark whites and overly saturated pinks, favoring sun-warmed alabaster, dusty botanicals, and deep mineral pigments. 

### Palette Application

- **Background & Canvas**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Ivory) forms the tactile substrate.
  - `Raised Surface`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Porcelain) provides crisp separation for cards and modal sheets.
  - `Subtle Field`: `#FDFBF7` (Silk Sheen) softens high-density form areas and module backgrounds.

- **Primary & Accent Tones**:
  - `Primary (#C47C85)`: Muted vintage rose. Used for high-intent primary interactions, selected states, and pivotal graphic accents. For deep hover or focus states, darken to `#A85A65`.
  - `Secondary Accent (#E8B4B8)`: Powder blush. Serves as active fills, pill badge backdrops, and interactive state transitions. Sub-tints include `#ECCFD3` and `#F5E6E8`.
  - `Tertiary Accent (#D8CEEB)`: Iridescent lavender sheen. Reserved for AI-driven highlights, algorithmic generative tags, scan animations, and luminous micro-indicators. Pair with `#EBE4F5` for delicate halo gradients.

- **Neutrals & Text**:
  - `Neutral Dark (#241E20)`: Deep warm espresso. Applied to editorial headlines, display titles, and prominent high-contrast text. Never use pure black `#000000`.
  - `Neutral Mid (#655B5E)`: Cashmere taupe. Applied to secondary body text, captions, and non-active icons.
  - `Neutral Light / Hairlines (#F0E5E7)`: Rose-tinted champagne borders that provide whisper-quiet structure without segmenting the canvas aggressively. Alternate subtle separator: `#EAE0DF`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence. The dialogue between the neoclassical curves of `Playfair Display` and the contemporary geometric clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans` reflects the intersection of classical beauty rituals and modern diagnostics.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Used for brand moments, diagnostic section milestones, product names, and editorial pull-quotes. It must maintain comfortable tracking and never be rendered in faux-italics or excessively heavy weights.
- **Interface & Reading (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers supreme clarity at small scales for formulation breakdowns, routine step guides, skin-metric readouts, and form labels.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: Uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) is used selectively for tags, categorization headers, routine timestamps, and ingredient badges to convey catalogued elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the deliberate breathing room of luxury editorial print publications. Negative space is treated as an active surface component rather than unused screen real estate.

- **Grid Framework**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `4rem` outer margin, `2.5rem` gutters. Content blocks max out at a refined `1280px` centered enclosure to preserve scanning comfort.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `2.5rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Full-width visual elements bleed softly with nested container paddings.

- **Spacing Cadence**:
  - Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal element relationships, such as label-to-input gaps and micro-badges.
  - Use `space-md` (16px) for intra-component flow and tight groupings.
  - Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding and related module separations.
  - Use `space-xl` (40px) and above to isolate major diagnostic segments, product curations, and routine chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation rejects harsh directional drops and stark multi-tiered card stacking. Depth is instead rendered through atmospheric diffusion, frosted transparency, and champagne-rose ambient glows that evoke softly lit dressing tables and polished vanity glass.

- **The Ambient Luxury Shadow**:
  - Base shadow: `0 10px 30px -5px rgba(196, 124, 133, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 30, 32, 0.03)`
  - Elevated modal/drawer shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(196, 124, 133, 0.14), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(36, 30, 32, 0.04)`
- **Glassmorphic Tiers**:
  - Translucent surfaces use `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` supported by a hardware-accelerated `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`.
  - Floating controls and sticky consultation headers float above ivory backdrops using delicate single-pixel champagne boundaries: `border: 1px solid rgba(240, 229, 231, 0.8)`.
- **Iridescent AI Shimmer**:
  - Special computational beauty assessments, shade matches, and diagnostic cards employ a subtle underglow: a multi-stop pseudo-element blur containing `#EBE4F5` and `#F5E6E8` at 40% opacity, providing a quiet, high-tech luminescence.

## Shapes

The shape system is sculpted, organic, and soft to the touch, echoing rounded cosmetic compacts, serum droplets, and smooth glass jars.

- **Scale Application**:
  - **Inputs, Cards & Surface Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px to `rounded-xl` / 24px)**: Generously radiused to eliminate rigid industrial sharpness while maintaining structural discipline.
  - **Pill Badges, Buttons & Interactive Tags (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Full pill geometry applied to CTA elements, routine steps, shade tags, and category chips.
  - **Sheet & Modal Drawers**: Mobile and desktop consultation sheets feature `24px` top radiused corners, creating an enveloping, protective tray gesture.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Signature)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `background: #C47C85`, text `color: #FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#A85A65` with a subtle elevation expansion: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(168, 90, 101, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Action (Glass Pill)**: Pill-shaped, `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`, `border: 1px solid #EAE0DF`, `color: #241E20`. Hover background transitions smoothly to `#FAF8F5`.
- **AI Consultation CTA (Luminous)**: Pill-shaped, subtle dual-tone gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #F5E6E8 0%, #EBE4F5 100%)`, text `color: #241E20`, bordered with an iridescent hairline `1px solid rgba(216, 206, 235, 0.6)`.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Status & Ingredient Chips**: Height `28px`, pill-shaped, padding `0 12px`. Light blush fill `background: #F5E6E8`, text `color: #A85A65`, uppercase tracking via `label-sm`.
- **AI Recommendation Tag**: Iridescent lavender backdrop (`background: #EBE4F5`), text `color: #584973`, accompanied by a minimal 12px metallic star/sparkle icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text & Camera Query Fields**: Height `48px`, `background: #FFFFFF`, `border: 1px solid #F0E5E7`, `border-radius: 12px`, padding `0 16px`. Typography: `body-md` in `#241E20`. 
- **Focus State**: Hairline shift to `1px solid #C47C85` supported by a diffuse rose ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(232, 180, 184, 0.25)`. No harsh black focus outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Radios are circular (20px), checkboxes are soft-rounded (6px). Checked state: fill `#C47C85` with pure white iconography. Unchecked state: `1.5px solid #EAE0DF`.

### Cards & Routine Modules
- **Product & Recommendation Cards**: `background: #FFFFFF`, `border: 1px solid #F0E5E7`, `border-radius: 20px`, padding `24px`. Shadow is strictly the ambient rose tint (`0 10px 30px -5px rgba(196, 124, 133, 0.08)`).
- **Interactive Hover**: Card translates vertically by `-2px` with a subtle increase in border illumination via `#ECCFD3`.

### Specialized Beauty Components
- **Shade Swatch Selector**: Circular swatches (32px diameter) encircled by a hairline ring with a 3px transparent gap on active state (`ring: 2px solid #C47C85`, `ring-offset: 3px`).
- **Diagnostic Metric Slider**: Minimal 4px track (`background: #F0E5E7`) with active fill in `#C47C85`. The slider thumb is an 18px porcelain circle with a centered muted rose core and ambient drop shadow.
- **Skin Analysis Glass Sheet**: Frosted floating overlay featuring `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, highlighting facial zones with champagne gold reticles (`#D4AF37` at 0.5 opacity) and soft pulse transitions.
